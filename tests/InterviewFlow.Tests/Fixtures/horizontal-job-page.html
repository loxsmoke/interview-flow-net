JOB DESCRIPTION

Join Our Innovative Team as a Software Engineer

Are you passionate about building scalable, resilient systems that push the boundaries of technology? We’re seeking a talented Software Engineer to be a key player in transforming data infrastructure and advancing our long-term engineering goals. If you're eager to work in a dynamic, inclusive environment where your skills make a real impact, this is your opportunity to thrive.

What You’ll Do:

  • Lead the migration of complex data systems from PostgreSQL to DynamoDB.
  • Design, deploy, and optimize highly available architectures using Terraform.
  • Contribute to shaping the company’s technical roadmap and architecture initiatives.
  • Collaborate seamlessly across cross-functional teams, including engineering, Product, and Program Management.
  • Develop robust microservices using Kotlin and Python to meet high-performance standards.

Required Skills:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or 7+ years of relevant experience.
  • Proven track record shipping large-scale backend services to production.
  • Expertise in Kotlin, Spring Boot, REST API design, and databases.
  • Strong background in cloud infrastructure, including Kubernetes, Docker, and AWS services such as DynamoDB, IAM, and PostgreSQL.
  • Exceptional problem-solving and debugging skills.

Nice to Have Skills:

  • Experience in transportation, robotics, logistics, or autonomous vehicle sectors.
  • Familiarity with protocol buffers (protobufs), gRPC, and LLM-based coding tools like Claude Code.
  • Knowledge of observability frameworks like OpenTelemetry and Grafana.

Preferred Education and Experience:

  • A degree in Computer Science or equivalent.
  • Extensive hands-on experience with cloud-native architectures and multi-region deployments.

Other Requirements:

  • Ability to work onsite in Foster City, CA.
  • Successful completion of background checks and drug screening.
